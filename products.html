Our Products

Five proprietary platforms built to solve real operational challenges — ready to deploy, ready to scale.

Notifications

Blendys

Blendys is a smartphone notification platform built for businesses that need to communicate with customers at scale. Send bulk notifications for order completions, service readiness, appointment reminders, or any custom trigger — all with your own branding and scheduling controls.

Available on both the App Store and Google Play, Blendys brings enterprise-grade push notification capabilities to businesses of any size with minimal integration effort.

App Store Google Play Custom Branding Scheduled Sends Bulk Notifications
Marketing

Viral Marketing Master

VMM (Viral Marketing Master) is a social network marketing platform that turns your existing customers into a powerful acquisition channel. The platform enables person-to-person recommendations with a built-in customer referral rewards system.

By making it easy and incentivising for satisfied customers to recommend your services, VMM creates a self-sustaining growth loop that drives new business without traditional advertising spend.

P2P Recommendations Referral Rewards Social Integration Campaign Analytics
Integration

Epic

Epic — the Enterprise Platform for Integration & Connectivity — is a middleware solution designed to connect disparate systems across your organisation. Whether on-premise or in the cloud, Epic creates a reliable data and process backbone that eliminates silos and enables seamless information flow.

Built for enterprise-scale requirements, Epic supports complex integration scenarios with high throughput, fault tolerance, and comprehensive monitoring.

On-Premise Cloud API Management High Throughput Fault Tolerant
Automation

SOL

SOL (Smart Object Listing) is an automated business process application that streamlines compliance, customer management, and operational oversight. It includes customer and device blacklisting, regulatory compliance tools, and full API integration with your existing systems.

SOL is designed for organisations that need to manage large, complex datasets of customers, devices, or assets with strict compliance and audit requirements.

Compliance Tools Blacklist Management API Integration Audit Trails
Location

Dallya Mobile Matching

Dallya is an indoor/outdoor location-based matching application that connects people and resources in proximity — intelligently and privately. Built with a privacy-first approach, Dallya uses anonymous profiles to facilitate matching without compromising user data.

Whether for event networking, proximity-based services, or smart logistics, Dallya's location intelligence engine delivers accurate, real-time matching that scales with your audience.

Indoor/Outdoor Anonymous Profiles Privacy First Real-Time Matching Location Intelligence
